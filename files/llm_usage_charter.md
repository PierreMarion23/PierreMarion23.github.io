# LLM Usage Charter 
V0.1 (Pierre Marion, 17/09/26, LLM L2)

## Four levels of assistance
+ L0, no LLM; 
+ L1, periphery (figures, boilerplate, asking the LLM to check small parts); 
+ L2, co-construction, the model proposes and I (re)write the core of the contribution myself; 
+ L3, delegation, the model produces and I audit.

## Research tasks
Research ideation, bibliographic search, code writing, mathematical derivation, paper writing.

## Charter

1.	I decide in advance which level of assistance is appropriate for a given project and a given research task. I keep part of my research activity with little or no assistance.
2.	I experiment with ways of prompting the model that keep me in full control of my scientific output and that build or maintain my own skills, rather than ways that maximise productivity.
3.	I am able to explain at the blackboard everything that goes into a paper, a talk, or research code.
4.	I check every reference the model suggests: the bibliographic data, and that the cited statement actually appears in the source.
5.	I do not expose confidential data to a commercial model. The scope of confidential data is to be agreed with my supervisor.
6.	I state the level of assistance proactively and systematically, by a statement on everything I produce and by declaration to my supervisor or student. The permitted level for each research task is revisited regularly with my supervisor. 
7.	I reread and rewrite anything I send to another person. I do not send walls of LLM-generated text.
